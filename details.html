Technical details

Practical details for May Institute Essentials 2026, which took place online November 2 - 6, 2026.

Session times

Sessions ran Monday, November 2 through Friday, November 6, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. Boston time (Eastern Standard Time, UTC−5). This is 15:00–17:30 UTC, or 7:00–9:30 a.m. PST, 3:00–5:30 p.m. GMT, and 4:00–6:30 p.m. CET.

Format

Sessions were held online as Zoom webinars, with webinar links emailed to registered participants each morning before the session.

Office Hours

Registered participants could sign up for 1:1 office hour slots with the developers of each tool.

Software and prerequisites

Sessions included code demos. The software, versions, and setup instructions used in each session can be found on the page for that session, linked from the schedule.

Certifications

Unfortunately we cannot offer certifications of attendance or participation for May Institute Essentials.
