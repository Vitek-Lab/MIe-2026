About

A shorter, online May Institute

May Institute Essentials is a condensed, fully virtual version of the May Institute, focused on the computational and statistical analysis of quantitative mass spectrometry–based proteomics. Over one week, sessions combine short lectures with general discussion, led by the developers of the methods and software they teach.

The program is intended for both newcomers and experienced researchers who would like to strengthen their computational and statistical expertise. We also welcome computer scientists, bioinformaticians, data scientists, statisticians, and engineers interested in working with data from modern biotechnologies. Sessions run each day from 10:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. Eastern Time.

Looking for the full, in-person program? See the regular May Institute.
