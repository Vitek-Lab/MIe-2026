About

Online, and just the essentials

May Institute Essentials is a condensed, fully virtual version of May Institute, focused on the essential building blocks of computational and statistical analysis of quantitative mass spectrometry–based proteomics and mass spectrometry imaging datasets. Over one week, sessions combine lectures with general discussion, led by the developers of the methods and software they teach.

The program is intended for early career researchers new to the field and tools as well as established scientists seeking to expand their expertise. Sessions run online each day from 10:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. Eastern Time. The schedule below outlines the topics we will be covering.

Looking for the full, in-person program? See the regular May Institute.
